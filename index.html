Distilling the Latent Space of the Particle Transformer into Interpretable Physics Equations

The Particle Transformer’s latent space (its class token), written as if-statements on jet physics quantities. Jet quantities used

The Particle Transformer (ParT) sorts particle jets of the JetClass dataset into 10 classes: QCD (light quark or gluon), H→bb̄, H→cc̄, H→gg, H→4q, H→ℓνqq′, Z→qq̄, W→qq′, t→bqq′, t→bℓν. It decides from 128 numbers, its class token after the last LayerNorm, which one linear layer turns into the 10 class scores. Each of those 128 numbers is rewritten here as a sum of if-statements on physics quantities of the jet (jet-level quantities, ParT’s own per-particle inputs, summaries of its pair inputs), tuned so that the network’s own last layer, fed with them, makes the same decisions as the network, while each formula number stays close to the network’s.

The same method as for the JEDI-linear tagger: the setup that gave the highest agreement there (all quantities, tuned on the network’s probabilities), then the same without mass quantities.
