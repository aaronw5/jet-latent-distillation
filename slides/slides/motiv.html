Last-Layer Neurons Have a ReLU-Shaped Cutoff

Many of the network’s last-layer neurons are exactly 0 below a threshold of one observable and grow roughly linearly above it: the shape of a ReLU, max(0, a·(x − t)).

Left: network neuron 3 against x = Σᵢ pT,ᵢ ΔRᵢ / Σᵢ pT,ᵢ (260,000 test jets). Below x = 0.067, 97% of jets give exactly 0; one hinge explains R² = 0.77. Right: neurons 10 and 2 (JEDI-linear explorer page).

One observable alone does not describe most neurons. This suggests writing each neuron as a sum of such hinges in several observables, keeping the network’s last layer.
