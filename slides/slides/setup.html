JEDI-linear

JEDI-linear is a graph-network jet tagger whose cost is linear in the number of particles, quantized for FPGA inference in the real-time trigger. Its inputs are the pT, Δη and Δφ of the 8 or 64 hardest particles of a jet; its outputs are scores for five classes: gluon (g), light quark (q), W, Z and top (t).

Network

Particles (pT, η, φ)

particle layers, sum over particles

jet layers 64, 32

16 neurons hⱼ

last layer: 5 scores

Class scores s_c = b_c + Σⱼ Kⱼ꜀ hⱼ; the largest score gives the class. Accuracy on the test file: 65.69% (8 particles) and 80.94% (64 particles).

5 · η, φ relative to the jet axis; hls4ml LHC jet dataset, 150 particles [16]; github.com/calad0i/JEDI-linear
