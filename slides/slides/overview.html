Method Overview

Five steps. Steps 1, 2 and 5 produce and test the formula; steps 3 and 4 are optional and only make it smaller.

stepnamewhat it doesneeded?
1MARS fitwrites each network neuron as a sum of hinges (if-statements) of observablesrequired
2Joint tuningtrains all coefficients together, to the network’s output or to the true labelsrequired
3Pruningremoves if-statements that add little, retraining after each cutoptional
4Further simplificationremoves more if-statements and whole observables, refitting to keep the neuron valuesoptional
5Evaluationall scores on the 260,000 test jetsrequired

8 particles, same class as the network on test jets: step 1 alone 88.3%; after steps 2 and 3, tuned on the network, 90.5% with 782 if-statements; after step 4, 90.1% with 399.
