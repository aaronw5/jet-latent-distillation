Neuron 10: A Single If-Statement

399 formula (tuned on the network, smaller version of the 782), 8 particles

The if-statement ΣzΔR² > 0.0075 adds to neuron 10 only for wide jets, and the amount grows linearly with ΣzΔR² above the threshold. 

  • if ΣzΔR² > 0.0075: z += 274 × (ΣzΔR² − 0.0075)
  • ΣzΔR² = Σᵢ pTᵢΔRᵢ² / Σᵢ pTᵢ
