Last-Layer Neurons Approximate High-Level Observables

In sections of their range, the network’s last-layer neurons are linear functions of standard jet observables, although the network only receives the particles’ (pT, η, φ). Neuron 12 falls linearly with the jet mass and reaches 0 near 90 GeV; neuron 2 rises linearly with the logarithm of the total pT above a threshold. Networks trained on particle-level inputs were found to learn quantities close to jet mass, N-subjettiness and energy correlations [2–4, 7, 8], and their decisions were mapped to energy-flow polynomials [5, 6].
