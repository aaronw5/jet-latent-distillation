Circuit explorer: how ParT makes each decision

ParT_full on JetClass. For each decision between two classes, the formula names a small circuit: a few attention heads that pick particles, and one to three neurons that turn what they read into the two class scores. Pick a decision and a jet; ParT runs in your browser (ONNX, the official weights) and the page shows where the circuit’s heads look in this jet, what its neurons read, and whether the circuit alone makes the same call. ← all pages
How a circuit is found. In the class-token formula (ParT’s attention × per-particle if-statements, ParT’s last layer) the score difference score(a) − score(b) is exactly a sum over the 16 heads and over the neurons. A head’s or neuron’s share is the covariance of its part with the total divided by the variance of the total, on 100,000 validation jets ParT calls a or b (shares sum to 1). The circuit is the top heads and the top 1–3 neurons.
How “circuit only” is run. The circuit’s neurons keep this jet’s values; every other neuron is fixed at its average over 20,000 validation jets; ParT’s last layer (linear) gives the scores. This isolates the neurons: each circuit neuron is still computed by all of ParT. The heads are tested separately (copying them between jets; setting them to their average).
